How to read this — Every stage is tallied independently for the month its own Date-Entered field falls in; there is no per-opportunity join back to New Lead. Because deals take real time to move stage to stage, a month's later-stage counts are mostly fed by leads that entered in earlier months, so these are period activity ratios rather than true same-cohort conversion rates -- the same caveat that applies to the main dashboard's Funnel Conversion card. The "All" selection counts every stage entry including deals owned by someone outside the tracked rep list or left unassigned, so the individual reps can sum to less than All. Per-rep months often rest on single-digit counts, where one deal swings a percentage by tens of points -- read movement at this granularity as noise unless it is large and sustained.
